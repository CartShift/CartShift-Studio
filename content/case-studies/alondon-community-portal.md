## Context

Alondon is a long-running Hebrew publication for Israelis living in or visiting London and the UK. The platform combines news, magazine content, guides, events, business information, and community resources.

## The Challenge

This was not an e-commerce redesign. The core problem was editorial: a content-heavy WordPress site needed clearer hierarchy, easier navigation, stronger mobile reading, and better separation between current stories, evergreen guides, and commercial content.

## The Implementation

- Adapted a new WordPress theme around editorial reading rather than product merchandising.
- Reworked information architecture across major sections and category paths.
- Improved Hebrew typography, spacing, navigation, and responsive behavior.
- Gave business and promotional content clearer entry points without letting it dominate the reading experience.
- Preserved the publication archive while presenting current content in a more modern system.

## What Changed

The portal now behaves more like a coherent digital publication. Readers can scan sections more quickly, move between editorial and practical content with less friction, and read comfortably on smaller screens. The result is primarily an information-architecture and editorial UX improvement, so the case study presents those changes directly rather than framing them as sales outcomes.
