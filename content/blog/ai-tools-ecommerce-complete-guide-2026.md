## Common AI Implementation Mistakes

### 1. Over-Automation Too Fast

**The Mistake:** Turning on every AI feature at once, losing control.

**The Fix:**

- Implement one category at a time
- Keep human oversight initially
- Gradually increase automation as you trust it
- Always have kill switch ready

### 2. Not Training the AI

**The Mistake:** Expecting AI to work perfectly from day one.

**The Fix:**

- Feed AI your best historical data
- Provide brand guidelines and voice
- Review and correct AI outputs initially
- Most AI improves with feedback loops

### 3. Ignoring Human Touch

**The Mistake:** Letting AI handle everything, losing authenticity.

**The Fix:**

- Use AI for 80%, human polish for 20%
- High-value customers get personal attention
- Premium products deserve hand-crafted copy
- Complex issues escalate to humans

### 4. No Performance Tracking

**The Mistake:** Implementing AI without measuring impact.

**The Fix:**

- Set baseline metrics before AI
- Track KPIs weekly
- Calculate ROI per tool
- Kill underperforming implementations

### 5. Cheap Data Quality

**The Mistake:** Feeding AI bad data, getting bad outputs.

**The Fix:**

- Clean your customer data first
- Standardize product information
- Remove duplicates and errors
- AI is only as good as your data
