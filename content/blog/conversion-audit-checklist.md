When a store is getting traffic but not sales, the answer is rarely "try harder." The answer is usually "audit the path between attention and action."

## Start with these six checks

### 1. Speed

- [ ] Is the landing page usable in under three seconds on mobile?
- [ ] Are hero images or videos too heavy?
- [ ] Are apps delaying interactivity?

### 2. Trust

- [ ] Is shipping and returns information easy to find?
- [ ] Are payment options visible early enough?
- [ ] Do product pages look credible?

### 3. Mobile usability

- [ ] Are buttons easy to tap?
- [ ] Is navigation obvious?
- [ ] Are forms too painful to complete?

### 4. Product clarity

- [ ] Are benefits explained clearly?
- [ ] Is sizing, delivery, or usage information missing?
- [ ] Are visuals doing enough selling work?

### 5. Cart and checkout friction

- [ ] Are there surprise costs?
- [ ] Is the flow longer than it needs to be?
- [ ] Are coupon or shipping expectations interrupting the decision?

### 6. Intent mismatch

- [ ] Does the page match the promise made in the ad, search result, or email?
- [ ] Are you attracting the wrong traffic?
- [ ] Are you ranking for terms that do not align with the offer?
