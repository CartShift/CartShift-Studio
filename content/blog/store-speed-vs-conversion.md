Page speed matters because it changes behavior.

Slow pages create doubt. Fast pages create flow.

## Why speed affects conversion

- users can act before they lose patience
- trust stays higher when the experience feels stable
- mobile users are less likely to bounce
- product and offer pages can do their job before friction takes over

## Common speed problems we see on Shopify

- oversized media
- too many apps
- duplicated scripts
- heavy popups that fire too early
- sections designed without performance review

## When speed is also an SEO issue

If your store is slow, you often see two problems at the same time:

1. weaker rankings or poor competitiveness on mobile
2. lower conversion rates from the traffic you do get

That is why we never treat SEO and conversion work as totally separate tracks.
